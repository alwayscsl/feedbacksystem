Course Registration

Need help registering for your courses? Follow the steps below.

1

Find Your Course

Go to your school's website and open the course page for the course you want to register for.

2

Find the Registration Link

Your course's registration link is usually in the syllabus or the syllabus quiz from your instructor.

3

Register for the Course

Click the registration link or paste it into your browser. You may need to log in before registering.

Submitting Assignments

Follow these steps to submit an assignment through Feedback System.

1

Open the Assignments Page

Go to the Assignments page to see the assignments for each class you are enrolled in.

2

Select Your Course

Choose your course from the Course dropdown on that page.

3

Upload Your Assignment

Click Upload next to the assignment, then choose your ZIP file and click Submit Assignment.

Feedback Results

Learn how Feedback System checks your assignment.

When you submit an assignment, it is compared to the correct code for that assignment. Steps you completed correctly show a success status. Steps that don't match the expected code show an error.

Check your file name

Your ZIP file must be named exactly as shown on the Upload page, or your submission will fail.

You can resubmit!

Submit as many times as you like until every step shows success.
